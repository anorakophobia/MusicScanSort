OCR models
----------
The embedded text-detection.onnx and text-recognition.onnx models are published
by the ocrs-models project:
https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs-models

The project states that its models are trained exclusively on open datasets,
including HierText, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The ocrs Rust engine is licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0:
https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs

PDFium
------
The executable embeds PDFium via pdfium-bundled. PDFium is distributed by the
pdfium-binaries project:
https://github.com/bblanchon/pdfium-binaries

PDFium's license and notices are available from:
https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/+/refs/heads/main/LICENSE
